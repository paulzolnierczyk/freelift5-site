# Support

FreeLift5 is a barbell training log that plans each workout for you. It needs no account and
keeps everything on your phone.

## Getting help

Open an issue at
[github.com/paulzolnierczyk/freelift5-site/issues](https://github.com/paulzolnierczyk/freelift5-site/issues)
with what you did, what you expected and what happened instead, plus your phone model and the
app version (Settings, at the bottom). Please don't attach a backup file or an export of your
history: they hold your training data, and issues are public.

## Common questions

**Where is my data?** Only on your phone, in the app's own storage. The developer never receives
it. Your phone's iCloud or Google backup includes it.

**How do I move to a new phone?** Restore your phone from its backup, or save a backup file in
Settings → Backup on the old phone and restore it on the new one.

**Can I bring my history from another app?** Yes, from a StrongLifts CSV export: Settings →
Import from StrongLifts. The app shows a preview before anything is saved. StrongLifts is a
trademark of its owner. FreeLift5 is not affiliated with or endorsed by StrongLifts.

**The plates don't add up to the weight on screen.** The app loads the bar with the plates you
told it you own (Settings → Units and equipment). When they can't make a weight exactly, it
shows the nearest one below and how far short it is.

**I missed reps. What happens now?** Each program has its own rules: most repeat the weight, and
take some off after repeated misses. The summary after each workout says what happens next time.

**Can I stay at a weight for a while?** Yes. On the Program screen, choose a lift's Change
progression and hold it at its weight, or change how much and how often it goes up.

**I took time off.** After seven days or more, the app offers a lighter restart when you come
back. You can take it, choose another amount, or keep your weights.

**Is this medical advice?** No. FreeLift5 is a training log. Check with a doctor before starting a
new program, especially with an injury or health condition. The weights are a guide: lift only
what you can lift safely, with good form. Use safety bars or a spotter. You are responsible for
how you train.
