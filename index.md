# FreeLift5

A barbell training log that does the arithmetic for you. Pick a program, enter the weights you
can lift today, and FreeLift5 plans every workout: the weight for each lift, the plates to put
on each side of the bar, your warm-ups and your rest.

- Six classic programs: 5x5, Starting Strength, Greyskull LP, Madcow 5x5, Texas Method and 5/3/1
- Plates per side from the plates you own, fractional plates included
- History, charts and a lighter restart after time off
- No account, no ads, no tracking: everything stays on your phone

Coming to the App Store; Google Play to follow.
