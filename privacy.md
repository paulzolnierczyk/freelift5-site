# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 30 September 2026_

FreeLift5 is a barbell training log for iPhone and Android, made by Paul Zolnierczyk. This policy
covers the app and this website.

## The short version

FreeLift5 collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
The app has no servers and sends nothing to the developer or anyone else. What you enter stays
on your phone.

## What the app keeps, and where

The app stores what you enter so it can plan your training: your program, the weights and sets
you log, your bodyweight and notes if you add them, your equipment and your settings. All of it
is kept in the app's own storage on your phone. The developer never receives any of it and has
no way to see it.

- **Your phone's backup.** If iCloud Backup (iPhone) or Google backup (Android) is on, your
  phone includes the app's data in that backup, as it does for most apps. That backup belongs
  to your Apple or Google account, under their privacy terms; the developer has no access to it.
- **Backup files you save.** Settings → Backup can save everything to a file. The file goes only
  where you choose to send it with your phone's share sheet.
- **Files you import.** When you import a CSV export from another app or restore a backup, the
  app reads the file you pick on the phone itself. Nothing is uploaded.

## Permissions

- **Notifications**, to tell you when your rest between sets is up. These are scheduled on the
  phone; nothing is sent from a server.

The app asks for nothing else: not your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone or health
data.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Backup → Delete everything erases all of the app's data from the phone. Deleting the
app does the same. Copies in your phone's backups and in any backup files you saved are yours to
delete.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, children included.

## This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses,
under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics of its own.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. A change that would
collect data would come with a new version of the app and be described in its release notes.
